How it's measured

Scenario
A line to say, where to say it (Notes, Gmail, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude Code, a shell), the expected text and checks. The set covers self-corrections, negations, numbers, slang, stutters, lists, email layout and shell symbols.
Passed
Required phrases present, banned phrases absent, the right layout for the app, every negation and number kept, and the right mode chosen.
STT WER
Word error rate of what speech-to-text heard against the script, after normalizing case, punctuation and number spelling.
Output WER and CER
Word and character error rates of what was typed against the expected text, normalized the same way. They measure whether the right words reached the screen.
Strict CER
Character error rate with case, punctuation and line breaks kept. It measures layout: list items, email greeting and sign-off lines, command flags.
Novel words
Share of typed content words the speaker never said: rewording by the model, or a mishearing such as "love" for "lol".
Kept words
Share of the expected text's content words that were typed.
Latency
From releasing the key to the text being typed. Synthetic runs time speech-to-text plus cleanup, without the mic or typing.

Harness: bench/scenario_eval.py in the Aloud repository (run, replay, reclean, report, compare).
